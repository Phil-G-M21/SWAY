The Idea

Streetwear isn't defined by a zip code. It's defined by attitude. Born in Accra and built for the global underground, SWAY exists to disrupt the ordinary. We looked at the everyday essential tee and asked one question. What if it had a pulse?

We threw out the rulebook of standard fits. For women, our cropped tees sit intentionally above the hip. Sharp, structural, and unapologetic. For men, our waist-length silhouettes anchor an outfit with effortless weight and presence. This isn't fast fashion churned out by algorithms. It's deliberate design for people who move differently, no matter where they are in the world.

SWAY is an attitude toward how you move through the world. It's about breaking away from monotonous basics to embrace deliberate cuts, heavy textures, and symbolic storytelling. Our graphics explore the friction between control and chaos, time, and raw energy. When you wear SWAY, you're defining your entire aesthetic.

The Craft & The Culture

Every single piece starts as a blank canvas and is brought to life through hand-screen-printing in Ghana. We build heavier because true quality has weight. It holds its shape, breathes with you, and ages like a classic vintage find. We don't do tiny chest logos or forgettable basics. Every shirt features a full-back narrative. Wearable art that sparks conversations from Accra to anywhere you find yourself.

For the Culture, Across Generations

Great style doesn't have an age limit. Whether you've been collecting vintage streetwear for decades or you're defining your aesthetic for the first time, SWAY speaks a universal language. It's for the risk-takers, the late-night creators, and anyone who refuses to blend into the background.

This is SWAY. Crafted in Accra. Worn worldwide.

Shipping

Where we ship

SWAY ships from Accra to everywhere in Ghana, across Africa, and worldwide. Every order is packed and sent from our base in Ghana.

Delivery times

Accra: 1 to 2 business days. Kumasi and other regions: 2 to 4 business days. Rest of Africa: 5 to 10 business days. International: 7 to 14 business days. Times are estimates and can vary during drops and holidays.

Shipping cost

Standard shipping within Ghana is free on orders over GHS 600. Below that, a flat rate is added at checkout. Express delivery is available in Accra and Kumasi for an added fee.

Tracking

Once your order ships you get a confirmation with tracking where available. For anything urgent, reach us on WhatsApp and we will chase it down.

Returns & Exchanges

Our promise

We want you in the right fit. If something is not right, you have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it.

Condition

Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with any tags still attached. Tees that have been worn or washed cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

How to start a return

Message us on WhatsApp or email with your order number and what you would like to do. We will send you the return steps. Return shipping within Ghana is on us for exchanges; for refunds the return cost is covered by the customer.

Refunds

Once we receive and check your return, refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. MoMo refunds are usually faster.

Wrong or faulty items

If you received the wrong item or a fault, tell us within 48 hours of delivery and we will make it right at no cost to you.
